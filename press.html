Session 5: Sparking America’s Global Economic & Climate Leadership with a National Green Bank - June 2021


Countries around the world have used national development banks and green banks to spark domestic economic growth and private sector investment. These institutions enable countries to increase their economic influence and competitiveness abroad by expanding local industries and exporting new technologies, business models and know-how. To explore this topic U.S. Senator Chris Van Hollen, a leading sponsor of the U.S. national green bank proposal, along with global development bank leaders will discuss how a national green bank in the U.S. can learn from other countries and spark new global leadership.
