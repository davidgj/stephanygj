Welcome

Welcome to the website of Professor Stephany Griffith-Jones (BA Chile, PhD Cambridge), Professor in Practice for Financing Sustainable Development at the SOAS Centre for Sustainable Finance, London University. She is also Emeritus Professorial Fellow at the Institute of Development Studies, Sussex University, and a Non-Resident Fellow at the Center for Global Development in Washington, DC.

She recently completed her tenure as Vice-President of the Board of the Central Bank of Chile, a position she held from January 2024 to December 2025. She was appointed to the Board in 2022.

Previously, she served as Financial Markets Director at the Initiative for Policy Dialogue at Columbia University and as a Professorial Fellow at the Institute of Development Studies. She was Deputy Director of International Finance at the Commonwealth Secretariat and has worked at the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs and the United Nations Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean.

Her research has focused on global capital flows, particularly flows to emerging markets; macroeconomic management of capital flows in Latin America, Eastern Europe, and sub-Saharan Africa; domestic financial-sector development; and financial regulation and reform of the international financial architecture. More recently, she has focused on monetary policy and development finance, particularly public development banks.

She has published more than 25 books and written many scholarly and journalistic articles.
